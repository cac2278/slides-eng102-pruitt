Why are op-eds hard to find?

Op-eds have an identity problem.

Different newspapers—and different databases—may use different labels for opinion writing.

Guest Essay

Guest Opinion

Opinion

Op-Ed

Commentary

Column

Don’t rely on the label alone. Look for a named author making an argument and supporting it with reasons or evidence.

Watch out: an Editorial usually represents the newspaper or editorial board; a Letter to the Editor is a reader response.
